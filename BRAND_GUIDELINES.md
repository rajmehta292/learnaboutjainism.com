# Learn About Jainism: Brand & Design Language

**Core Philosophy:** Reverence without intimidation. Depth without gatekeeping.
**Vibe:** A quiet derasar, a marble courtyard, a carefully preserved manuscript. Stillness, warmth, and intellectual seriousness.

## 1. Color System (Tailwind Configuration)
The palette is warm, natural, restrained, and highly readable. 

**Primary Colors:**
- **Marble Canvas (`#F6F3EC`)**: The main page background. Warm, off-white.
- **Paper Surface (`#FCFAF5`)**: For reading surfaces, elevated content, source panels.
- **Soft Stone (`#E9E3D8`)**: For secondary backgrounds and quiet section changes.
- **Carved Line (`#D5CCBD`)**: For borders and dividers.
- **Temple Ink (`#2E2C28`)**: Primary text color (avoid pure black).
- **Quiet Ink (`#625D55`)**: Secondary text, metadata, supporting explanations.

**Accent Colors (Use Sparingly):**
- **Aged Brass (`#9B7447`)**: Links, active states, source markers.
- **Compassion Leaf (`#66705F`)**: Verified states, gentle highlights.
- **Manuscript Earth (`#806A55`)**: Historical and archival material.
- **Quiet Slate (`#626A70`)**: Neutral informational states.

## 2. Typography
- **Headings / Long-form Reading:** Noto Serif or Source Serif (for scripture, philosophy).
- **Interface / Metadata:** Noto Sans or Inter (for clear UI).
- **Diacritics:** Support scholarly transliteration but don't force it on beginners.

## 3. UI and Motion
- **Motion:** Stillness is the default. Animations should feel like a page settling or light entering a courtyard (soft opacity fades, max 480ms duration). No bouncing, popping, or gamification.
- **Cards:** Very subtle. Thin borders, slight tonal contrast, minimal shadow.
- **Emojis:** **DO NOT USE.** Use custom monoline SVG icons or clean typography instead.
- **Aesthetics:** No high-gloss gold, neon accents, heavy glassmorphism, or AI sparkles. 

## 4. Editorial Voice
- **Tone:** A patient teacher + a careful librarian + a welcoming host.
- **Rule:** Attribute claims (e.g., "Jain texts describe..."). Avoid "Jainism says..." or "This is scientifically proven...". 
- **AI Positioning:** The AI is an invisible librarian ("Ask the Library"), not a "JainGPT" oracle.

## 5. Architectural Metaphor
- **The Threshold:** The Homepage (Calm, orienting, spacious).
- **The Courtyard:** Whitespace (Breathing room between ideas).
- **The Shrine:** Focus on the subject (No distracting CTAs).
- **The Bhandar:** The Library (Precise, bibliographic, trustworthy).
