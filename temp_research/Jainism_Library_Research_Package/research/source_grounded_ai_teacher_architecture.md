# Source-Grounded Jain Study Guides and Advice System

Generated: 2026-07-22

## Authority boundary
The system must never claim to be a Maharaj Saheb, Acharya, monk, nun, guru, or source of binding religious rulings. Expert depth must come from verified primary texts, reviewed commentaries, tradition-aware retrieval, and qualified human review.

## Answer pipeline
1. Classify topic, tradition, risk, depth, and whether the user wants information or action.
2. Retrieve verified primary passages first.
3. Add commentaries and scholarship only under the selected mode.
4. Map each material claim to a source.
5. Display disagreements, missing evidence, and translation uncertainty.
6. Separate quotation, paraphrase, interpretation, and modern application.
7. Apply authority, medical, mental-health, legal, financial, and coercion checks.
8. Show direct citations and recommend a qualified human when needed.

## Mandatory escalation
- Self-harm, suicide, or sallekhana requests
- Prolonged or waterless fasting; illness; pregnancy; childhood; diabetes; eating disorders; medication
- Abuse, coercion, forced practice, or family violence
- Diksha or abandonment of dependents
- Medical, mental-health, legal, or financial decisions
- Questions requiring authorization from a living lineage

## Corpus tiers
- A: verified primary text and edition
- B: reviewed traditional commentary
- C: peer-reviewed or authoritative scholarship
- D: reviewed community education
- E: unverified OCR or AI translation, discovery only
- F: unknown provenance, quarantined
